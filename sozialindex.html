Hamburger Sozialindex

The Hamburg Social Index is a tool used to measure the different social conditions of schools across Hamburg. It combines various indicators, such as the socioeconomic background of students, educational support needs, and local living conditions, to estimate the challenges schools face in their everyday work. Based on these factors, schools are assigned to different social index levels, which help identify where additional resources and support are needed most. The index is an important instrument because it shows that educational opportunities are not shaped by schools alone, but also by the environments in which students grow up. By making social differences visible, the Social Index enables a more equal distribution of resources and supports schools in providing every student with better opportunities for educational success.
